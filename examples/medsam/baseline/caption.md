Original editable explanatory redraw of MedSAM (Ma et al., 2024; DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-44824-z), based on Figure 2b and Methods, not a reproduction of patient images. (a) Training uses 2D images and boxes derived from expert masks with 0–20 pixel perturbation. The image encoder and mask decoder are updated; the prompt encoder stays frozen. Expert masks supervise the unweighted Dice + binary cross-entropy (BCE) loss. The AdamW update is summarized in text, not drawn as a data-flow arrow. (b) Inference uses a user box and image, without an expert target mask. Solid arrows indicate computation/prompt derivation and dashed arrows supervision, not causal clinical effects. The lower network box abstracts the same trained components. No performance data, clinical benefit or new training is claimed. Generic 180 mm export; target-journal requirements not checked.
